YouTube Manager — Settings

Managing a lot of bookmarks? Open the full library in its own tab for more room than the popup.

In-page panel

Turn off to hide the bookmark panel, playlist panel, and seek-bar markers on YouTube video pages. Your bookmarks and tags are kept and still sync in the background — this only hides the UI. This preference syncs across your devices, same as Autoplay.

GitHub Gist sync

Create a token at github.com/settings/tokens with only the gist scope. The token is stored locally in this browser (chrome.storage.local) and is only ever sent to api.github.com.

Turn off to only sync when you click "⟲ Sync" yourself. This preference syncs across your devices too.

Danger zone

Discards every bookmark, tag, and preference stored in this browser and reloads everything fresh from the Gist configured above. Anything local that hasn't made it to the Gist yet (e.g. from a device that hasn't synced) is lost — nothing on GitHub is touched. Use this if local data here looks wrong and you just want to trust the Gist.
