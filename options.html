GitHub Gist sync

Create a token at github.com/settings/tokens with only the gist scope. The token and Gist ID are stored in chrome.storage.sync — tied to your signed-in Chrome/Google account, not just this browser — so signing into the same account on another device or browser fills these in automatically and that device starts pulling your bookmarks from the same Gist. They're only ever sent to api.github.com otherwise.

Turn off to only sync when you click "⟲ Sync" yourself. This preference syncs across your devices too.

Use this if local data here looks wrong and you just want to trust the Gist.
